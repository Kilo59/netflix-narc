# Welcome to netflix-narc

**netflix-narc** is a modern terminal UI (TUI) and CLI tool designed specifically for parents, guardians, and caregivers to monitor and evaluate Netflix viewing history against customizable safety criteria.

## Why netflix-narc?

Keeping track of what children watch on streaming services can be challenging. **netflix-narc** simplifies this by:
- Automatically parsing your exported Netflix Viewing History CSV.
- Fetching title metadata and age ratings from trusted external providers like **Common Sense Media**, **OMDb**, and **TMDB**.
- Flagging content based on your personal severity weights for categories such as violence, language, sex/nudity, and drug/alcohol use.
